# How to name question fields

Question titles identify answers in <span className="text-cyan-500 font-bold bg-cyan-100 px-2 py-1 rounded">Summary</span> and <span className="text-cyan-500 font-bold bg-cyan-100 px-2 py-1 rounded">Submissions</span>, and naming fields is free on every plan. Enter a title in a question’s <span className="text-cyan-500 font-bold bg-cyan-100 px-2 py-1 rounded">Label</span> section or ask AI to name it.

Makeform is a free AI form builder: describe what you want to collect and it builds the form for you, with unlimited forms and submissions on the free plan.

## Create question titles
You can tell AI the name of question titles you want to create.  
Question titles are **not** automatically created in your forms when you manually add question titles.

To manually add question titles:
 - Insert a question
 - Enter the title in <span className="text-cyan-500 font-bold bg-cyan-100 px-2 py-1 rounded">Label</span> section
