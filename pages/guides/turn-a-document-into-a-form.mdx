# How to turn a PDF, Word file, or photo into a form

Attach your document or photo in Makeform's AI chat and ask it to build a form from the file. This is free at https://www.makeform.ai/create without an account, or in your form editor's chat.

**Is it free?** Yes. Turning a supported document or photo into a form is free on every plan.

## Steps

1. Open https://www.makeform.ai/create or the chat box in your form editor. There is no separate importer.
2. Click the <span className="text-cyan-500 font-bold bg-cyan-100 px-2 py-1 rounded">+</span> attach button, or drag your file into the chat.
3. Type a request such as “Turn this into an online form. Keep the same questions and make name and email required.”
4. Send your message to have the AI build the form.
5. Check every question against your original document. Fix anything by clicking the question or asking the AI to change it.
6. Sign in if you started without an account, then click <span className="text-cyan-500 font-bold bg-cyan-100 px-2 py-1 rounded">Publish</span> to publish the form.

## Supported files and limits

You can attach PDFs, Word `.docx` files, and images in PNG, JPG/JPEG, WebP, or HEIC/HEIF format.

- Attach up to 5 files per message.
- Images and PDFs can be up to 1.5 MB each, with a 1.5 MB total limit for images and PDFs.
- PDFs can have up to 5 pages.
- Word `.docx` files can be up to 4 MB.

<Callout type="info">
Old `.doc` files are not supported. Export the document as a PDF or save it as `.docx`, then upload it.
</Callout>

## Use a photo or pasted questions

A photo of a paper form works. Make sure the text is sharp and the whole page is in frame, then check the generated questions against the photo.

You can also paste a list of questions as text and ask the AI to turn it into a form. For example, copy the questions from a Google Form or a document into the chat.

## FAQ

### Can I import a Google Form, Typeform, or URL directly?

No, there is no direct import from Google Forms, Typeform, or a URL. You can paste a list of questions as text instead.

### Can I upload a CSV or Excel spreadsheet?

No, CSV and Excel spreadsheets are not accepted as a form source. Use a supported document or image, or paste your questions as text.

### Do I need an account before uploading a document?

No, you can start at https://www.makeform.ai/create without an account. You need to sign in before publishing the form.

### How do I fix a question after conversion?

Click the question to edit it, or ask the AI to change it. Check every question against your original before publishing.
