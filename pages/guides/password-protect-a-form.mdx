# How to password protect a form in Makeform

Password protection requires respondents to enter a password before opening and submitting your form. It is free on every plan in <span className="text-cyan-500 font-bold bg-cyan-100 px-2 py-1 rounded">Settings</span> > <span className="text-cyan-500 font-bold bg-cyan-100 px-2 py-1 rounded">Access</span>.

**Is it free?** Yes. Password protection is included on every plan.

## Steps

1. Open your form and click the <span className="text-cyan-500 font-bold bg-cyan-100 px-2 py-1 rounded">Settings</span> icon in the top bar.
2. In <span className="text-cyan-500 font-bold bg-cyan-100 px-2 py-1 rounded">Access</span>, turn on <span className="text-cyan-500 font-bold bg-cyan-100 px-2 py-1 rounded">Password protection</span>.
3. Enter a password of 8–128 characters. Passwords are case-sensitive.
4. Click <span className="text-cyan-500 font-bold bg-cyan-100 px-2 py-1 rounded">Save Changes</span>. Access changes take effect when saved; you do not need to publish.
5. Optionally, click <span className="text-cyan-500 font-bold bg-cyan-100 px-2 py-1 rounded">Preview password screen</span> to see the password screen.

Share the password with respondents separately from the form link. They need the password to open and submit the protected form.

## Find or change the saved password

Only the form owner can reveal and copy the saved password. Use <span className="text-cyan-500 font-bold bg-cyan-100 px-2 py-1 rounded">Reveal saved password</span> to see it or <span className="text-cyan-500 font-bold bg-cyan-100 px-2 py-1 rounded">Copy saved password</span> to copy it.

To replace the password, use <span className="text-cyan-500 font-bold bg-cyan-100 px-2 py-1 rounded">Change password</span>. People with access will need the new password, so share the replacement with the respondents who should still have access.

## Remove password protection

Turn off <span className="text-cyan-500 font-bold bg-cyan-100 px-2 py-1 rounded">Password protection</span>. In the <span className="text-cyan-500 font-bold bg-cyan-100 px-2 py-1 rounded">Remove password protection?</span> confirmation, choose <span className="text-cyan-500 font-bold bg-cyan-100 px-2 py-1 rounded">Remove protection</span>.

<Callout type="info">
After protection is removed, anyone with the link can open the form if it is published and open.
</Callout>

Removing the password changes who can open the form. If you want to stop accepting submissions, see [close form rules](/features/close-form) for closing the form now, on a scheduled date, or at a submission limit.

Makeform is a free AI form builder: describe what you want to collect and it builds the form for you, with unlimited forms and submissions on the free plan.

## FAQ

### Do I need to publish after setting a password?

No. Access changes take effect when you save them. Click <span className="text-cyan-500 font-bold bg-cyan-100 px-2 py-1 rounded">Save Changes</span> after setting up the password.

### How long does the browser remember the password?

After a respondent enters the password, their browser remembers it for 24 hours. The password itself is case-sensitive, so respondents need to enter the same uppercase and lowercase letters you used.

### Can a teammate reveal the saved password?

Only the form owner can reveal and copy it. The owner can use <span className="text-cyan-500 font-bold bg-cyan-100 px-2 py-1 rounded">Reveal saved password</span> or <span className="text-cyan-500 font-bold bg-cyan-100 px-2 py-1 rounded">Copy saved password</span> when they need to retrieve it later.

### Does a password stop someone from submitting twice?

Password protection requires a password to open and submit the form. To accept only one completed response for each value in a selected field, use [prevent duplicate submissions](/guides/prevent-duplicate-submissions).
