# How to create a form with AI from one sentence

Describe what you want to collect at https://www.makeform.ai/create, and Makeform builds a draft you can edit. You can start free without an account, then sign in to save and publish it.

**Is it free?** Yes. AI creation with the standard model, MF Mini, is free on every plan; Pro adds MF Pro, MF Next, and MF Ultra.

## Steps

1. Go to https://www.makeform.ai/create. If you are signed in, you can also click <span className="text-cyan-500 font-bold bg-cyan-100 px-2 py-1 rounded">Create Form</span> in the dashboard sidebar.
2. In the box labeled <span className="text-cyan-500 font-bold bg-cyan-100 px-2 py-1 rounded">What do you want to make?</span>, type one sentence. For example: “A volunteer sign-up form with name, email, shift preference and a waiver checkbox.”
3. Send your request. The AI writes a plan and builds the form in the same turn. If you asked to see the plan first, it waits for <span className="text-cyan-500 font-bold bg-cyan-100 px-2 py-1 rounded">Create this form</span> confirmation.
4. Review the draft on the canvas. Click fields to edit them, or keep chatting to request changes.
5. Click <span className="text-cyan-500 font-bold bg-cyan-100 px-2 py-1 rounded">Sign in to save &amp; publish</span> in the top bar. Sign in with Google or email; your draft and chat move into your account.
6. Click <span className="text-cyan-500 font-bold bg-cyan-100 px-2 py-1 rounded">Publish</span> at the top right to get the link.

## Write a useful prompt

Say who will fill in the form, what you need to collect, and any rules such as required fields, limits, or payment. Include what should happen after submission.

The AI can build multi-page forms, conditional logic, page routing, scoring, quizzes, calculations, several endings, redirects, and themes. You can also [attach a PDF, Word file, or photo](/guides/turn-a-document-into-a-form) as a starting point.

<Callout type="info">
Drafts without an account are kept for 1 hour. Guests can build, edit, and preview; publishing, downloads, settings, languages, and versions require sign-in.
</Callout>

## FAQ

### How many questions can my form have?

Free forms and forms created without an account can have up to 50 questions. Pro and Business forms can have up to 100.

### Do I have to approve a plan before the AI builds?

Usually, the AI plans and builds in the same turn. It only waits for confirmation if you asked to see the plan first.

### Can the AI generate images?

No, it cannot generate images. It can work on the form's design and themes.

### Can I change the form after it is generated?

Yes, click fields on the canvas or keep chatting with the AI. Review the draft before publishing it.
