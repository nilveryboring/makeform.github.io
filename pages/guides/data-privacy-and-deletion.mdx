# Makeform privacy and data: where responses are stored and how to delete them

Delete individual responses from <span className="text-cyan-500 font-bold bg-cyan-100 px-2 py-1 rounded">Submissions</span>, or request account deletion from <span className="text-cyan-500 font-bold bg-cyan-100 px-2 py-1 rounded">Settings</span> > <span className="text-cyan-500 font-bold bg-cyan-100 px-2 py-1 rounded">Danger zone</span>. The privacy policy does not name a storage region; Business adds controls to hide or mask sensitive answers from teammates.

**Is it free?** Free includes unlimited forms and submissions, subject to fair use; <span className="text-cyan-500 font-bold bg-cyan-100 px-2 py-1 rounded">Visibility control</span> for hiding or masking fields requires Business.

## Steps

To delete a submission:

1. Open your form and go to <span className="text-cyan-500 font-bold bg-cyan-100 px-2 py-1 rounded">Submissions</span>.
2. Click the row's trash icon, labeled <span className="text-cyan-500 font-bold bg-cyan-100 px-2 py-1 rounded">Delete</span>. In a team, you need permission to delete submissions.
3. Confirm <span className="text-cyan-500 font-bold bg-cyan-100 px-2 py-1 rounded">Are you sure you want to delete this submission?</span>

To request account deletion:

1. Open <span className="text-cyan-500 font-bold bg-cyan-100 px-2 py-1 rounded">Settings</span> and go to <span className="text-cyan-500 font-bold bg-cyan-100 px-2 py-1 rounded">Danger zone</span>.
2. Choose <span className="text-cyan-500 font-bold bg-cyan-100 px-2 py-1 rounded">Delete account</span>.
3. Confirm with <span className="text-cyan-500 font-bold bg-cyan-100 px-2 py-1 rounded">Delete my account</span>.

<Callout type="warning">
Account deletion permanently deletes your entire account, including all forms, submissions, and workspaces. The request is reviewed and processed within 7 business days, and you receive an email confirmation.
</Callout>

## Storage and retention

Data is encrypted at rest with AES-256 via Supabase and AWS, and in transit with TLS. Makeform does not sell your personal data.

Submission data is kept until you delete it or remove the form. Account data is kept while the account exists and for up to 12 months after termination, unless deleted earlier on request. Drafts made without an account are deleted after 1 hour.

For access or erasure requests, email `support@makeform.ai`. Makeform responds within 30 days. If you need a specific storage region or a data processing agreement, contact that address before collecting data.

## Connected services and sensitive answers

Third-party integrations you connect, such as Google Sheets, Notion, and Slack, receive the data you send them. Business <span className="text-cyan-500 font-bold bg-cyan-100 px-2 py-1 rounded">Visibility control</span> can hide or mask fields from teammates.

The Terms prohibit collecting payment card numbers in form fields. Use the Payment block, which uses Stripe, to collect payments.

## Cookies on public forms

Makeform does not show a cookie banner on public forms. Your own Google Analytics or Meta Pixel loads only if you add it; those tracking features require Pro or Business. Check what your own laws require.

Makeform is a free AI form builder: describe what you want to collect and it builds the form for you, with unlimited forms and submissions on the free plan.

## FAQ

### Which region stores my responses?

The privacy policy does not name a storage region. If you need a specific region, email `support@makeform.ai` before collecting data.

### How long does account deletion take?

Your request is reviewed and processed within 7 business days. You receive an email confirmation, and deletion permanently removes your account, forms, submissions, and workspaces.

### Can I request a copy of my data or ask for erasure?

Email `support@makeform.ai` for access or erasure requests. Makeform responds within 30 days.

### Can I hide sensitive answers from teammates?

Business includes <span className="text-cyan-500 font-bold bg-cyan-100 px-2 py-1 rounded">Visibility control</span> to hide or mask fields. In a team, deleting submissions also requires permission to delete them.
