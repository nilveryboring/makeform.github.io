# How to edit a form by chatting with AI

Use the AI chat at the bottom of your form editor to change questions, logic, design, and settings. It is free for signed-in users who can edit the form.

**Is it free?** Yes. AI editing is free for signed-in users with editing access; translations require Pro.

## Steps

1. Open the form from your dashboard to enter the editor.
2. Find the chat box at the bottom with the placeholder <span className="text-cyan-500 font-bold bg-cyan-100 px-2 py-1 rounded">What do you want to change?</span>.
3. Type a request, such as “Make phone optional and add a question asking how they heard about us.”
4. To change one field, click it on the canvas first. The box shows <span className="text-cyan-500 font-bold bg-cyan-100 px-2 py-1 rounded">Selected “&lt;label&gt;”</span>, and the placeholder becomes <span className="text-cyan-500 font-bold bg-cyan-100 px-2 py-1 rounded">Ask AI to change “&lt;label&gt;”…</span>, using that field's label.
5. Review the result on the canvas. You can also click any field to edit it by hand.
6. Click <span className="text-cyan-500 font-bold bg-cyan-100 px-2 py-1 rounded">Publish</span> at the top right when ready. Changes stay in the draft until you publish.

## Review changes and past messages

Click the <span className="text-cyan-500 font-bold bg-cyan-100 px-2 py-1 rounded">Conversation</span> pill or press ⌘K to see past messages. While the AI works, a bar shows <span className="text-cyan-500 font-bold bg-cyan-100 px-2 py-1 rounded">View progress</span>.

Removing content, payment changes, scoring changes, replacing custom CSS, settings changes, and some field type changes wait for your decision. Use <span className="text-cyan-500 font-bold bg-cyan-100 px-2 py-1 rounded">Confirm changes</span> or <span className="text-cyan-500 font-bold bg-cyan-100 px-2 py-1 rounded">Cancel changes</span> on the card.

## What you can ask the AI to change

You can request changes to questions, content, logic, design, settings, and email notifications. The AI can also work with email, Slack, webhook, Telegram, Google Sheets, and Notion integrations, and publish only after you confirm.

<Callout type="info">
One request can contain up to 200 edit actions. Review the resulting draft on the canvas before publishing.
</Callout>

## FAQ

### Can I ask the AI to change just one question?

Yes, click the field on the canvas before typing your request. The chat shows the selected field's label so you can direct the change to that field.

### Do AI edits immediately change the published form?

Changes are saved to the draft until you publish. When the AI handles publishing, it needs your confirmation.

### Can the AI set up my custom domain or generate images?

No, the AI cannot set up custom domains or generate images. Set up custom domains by hand.

### Can I turn an existing form into a printable document through chat?

No, AI editing cannot turn an existing form into a printable document. It also cannot convert a printable document back into a form.
