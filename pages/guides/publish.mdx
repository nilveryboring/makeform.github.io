# Publish your form

Click <span className="text-cyan-500 font-bold bg-cyan-100 px-2 py-1 rounded">Publish</span> to make your draft visible to respondents through your public link and embeds. The free plan includes unlimited forms and submissions.

Makeform is a free AI form builder: describe what you want to collect and it builds the form for you, with unlimited forms and submissions on the free plan.

Makeform keeps your draft separate from the version respondents see:

- Changes in the Builder update the **draft**.
- Preview shows the current draft.
- Your public form link and embeds continue showing the last **published** version.

When the draft is ready, click <span className="text-cyan-500 font-bold bg-cyan-100 px-2 py-1 rounded">Publish</span> to make it live. After making more changes, publish again before respondents can see them.

You can keep using the same public link and embed code after republishing.
