# How to print a form or download it as a PDF or Word file

Sign in and use <span className="text-cyan-500 font-bold bg-cyan-100 px-2 py-1 rounded">Download</span> in your form editor to open a printable copy of the form. You can print it or download a PDF or Word file for free on every plan.

**Is it free?** Yes. Printing and downloads are free for signed-in users; Free includes a Makeform band on each printed page.

## Steps

1. Sign in and open the form in the editor.
2. Click <span className="text-cyan-500 font-bold bg-cyan-100 px-2 py-1 rounded">Download</span> in the top bar to open the print preview.
3. Click <span className="text-cyan-500 font-bold bg-cyan-100 px-2 py-1 rounded">Customize</span>. Choose <span className="text-cyan-500 font-bold bg-cyan-100 px-2 py-1 rounded">Paper</span>: <span className="text-cyan-500 font-bold bg-cyan-100 px-2 py-1 rounded">Letter</span> or <span className="text-cyan-500 font-bold bg-cyan-100 px-2 py-1 rounded">A4</span>, then adjust writing space, color, and answer lines.
4. Click <span className="text-cyan-500 font-bold bg-cyan-100 px-2 py-1 rounded">Download PDF</span> or <span className="text-cyan-500 font-bold bg-cyan-100 px-2 py-1 rounded">Download DOCX</span> for Word. You can also print from the preview.

## Check what works on paper

File uploads, payments, calculations, and similar features cannot work on paper. The preview lists them under <span className="text-cyan-500 font-bold bg-cyan-100 px-2 py-1 rounded">Not on paper: ...</span> so you can review those parts of the form.

## Remove the branding band

On Free, each printed page has a small <span className="text-cyan-500 font-bold bg-cyan-100 px-2 py-1 rounded">Made with Makeform</span> band with a QR code at the bottom. Pro and Business can turn it off using <span className="text-cyan-500 font-bold bg-cyan-100 px-2 py-1 rounded">Remove Makeform branding</span> in <span className="text-cyan-500 font-bold bg-cyan-100 px-2 py-1 rounded">Customize</span>.

Free users are also offered a paid option to remove branding from one document, or a Pro pass.

## Print one person's answers

Open <span className="text-cyan-500 font-bold bg-cyan-100 px-2 py-1 rounded">Submissions</span> and click the row's <span className="text-cyan-500 font-bold bg-cyan-100 px-2 py-1 rounded">Export submission PDF</span> icon. Choose fields and style, then click <span className="text-cyan-500 font-bold bg-cyan-100 px-2 py-1 rounded">Print PDF</span>.

<Callout type="info">
Printing a submission opens the browser print dialog, where you can save it as a PDF. See [submission PDF export](/features/pdf-generation) for printing a person's answers.
</Callout>

## FAQ

### Can I download a form without an account?

You must sign in before downloading a printable copy. Downloads are free on every plan once you are signed in.

### Can I download a Word file instead of a PDF?

Yes, choose <span className="text-cyan-500 font-bold bg-cyan-100 px-2 py-1 rounded">Download DOCX</span> in the print preview. You can also choose PDF or print directly from the preview.

### Can I change the paper size and writing space?

Yes, open <span className="text-cyan-500 font-bold bg-cyan-100 px-2 py-1 rounded">Customize</span> to choose Letter or A4 paper. You can also adjust writing space, color, and answer lines.

### Will payments and calculations work on paper?

No, payments, calculations, and file uploads cannot work on paper. The preview lists those features so you can see what the printed form cannot do.
