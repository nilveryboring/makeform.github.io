# Tutorials

Follow these tutorials to build forms for specific tasks, from quizzes and order forms to booking requests and event registration. Each tutorial explains the setup and which features are free or require a paid plan.

Makeform is a free AI form builder: describe what you want to collect and it builds the form for you, with unlimited forms and submissions on the free plan.
