# Plans and pricing

Makeform offers a Free plan with unlimited forms and submissions, plus paid Pro and Business plans for additional features. Start free without an account at [makeform.ai/create](https://www.makeform.ai/create) and compare the plans below.

Makeform is a free AI form builder: describe what you want to collect and it builds the form for you, with unlimited forms and submissions on the free plan.

---

## Free Plan

Makeform is free to use with unlimited forms and submissions, subject to fair use.

**What's included in Free:**
- Unlimited forms and submissions
- All input types (30+ field types)
- Multi-page forms
- File uploads (20 MB per file)
- Self (owner) email notifications
- Conditional logic
- Form analytics
- Slack integration
- Embedded forms
- Basic AI assistance
- Standard support

<Callout type="info">
The Free plan is perfect for individuals, side projects, and small teams getting started with form building.
</Callout>

---

## Pro Plan

**$29/month** or **$19/month** (billed annually at $228, save 4 months)

Pro adds branding removal, custom CSS, premium AI models, and Google Analytics and Meta Pixel tracking.
