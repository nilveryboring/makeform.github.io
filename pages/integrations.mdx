# Integrations

Makeform integrations connect your forms to response apps, automation tools, AI agents, and the API. Use the guides below to connect Slack, Google Sheets, Notion, Zapier, Make, webhooks, or Telegram for free, or get started with Claude, the MCP server, and API keys.

Makeform is a free AI form builder: describe what you want to collect and it builds the form for you, with unlimited forms and submissions on the free plan.
