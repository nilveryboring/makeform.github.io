# How to add Google Analytics or Meta Pixel to a form in Makeform

Add Google Analytics 4 or Meta Pixel under your form's <span className="text-cyan-500 font-bold bg-cyan-100 px-2 py-1 rounded">Settings</span> > <span className="text-cyan-500 font-bold bg-cyan-100 px-2 py-1 rounded">Tracking</span> to track form activity. This feature requires Pro or Business; Meta Pixel works on hosted form links, not inside embeds.

**Is it free?** No. GA4 and Meta Pixel tracking require Pro or Business; dashboard response summaries are free.

## Steps

1. Open your form and go to <span className="text-cyan-500 font-bold bg-cyan-100 px-2 py-1 rounded">Settings</span> > <span className="text-cyan-500 font-bold bg-cyan-100 px-2 py-1 rounded">Tracking</span>.
2. Under <span className="text-cyan-500 font-bold bg-cyan-100 px-2 py-1 rounded">Google Analytics (GA4)</span>, paste your Measurement ID in the format `G-XXXXXXXXXX`. Find it in Google Analytics under <span className="text-cyan-500 font-bold bg-cyan-100 px-2 py-1 rounded">Admin</span> > <span className="text-cyan-500 font-bold bg-cyan-100 px-2 py-1 rounded">Data Streams</span>.
3. Under <span className="text-cyan-500 font-bold bg-cyan-100 px-2 py-1 rounded">Meta Pixel</span>, enter the Pixel ID from Meta Events Manager. You can add GA4, Meta Pixel, or both.
4. Click <span className="text-cyan-500 font-bold bg-cyan-100 px-2 py-1 rounded">Save Changes</span>.
5. Open the live form and check real-time reports in GA4 or the Meta Events Manager test tool.

## What each option tracks

<span className="text-cyan-500 font-bold bg-cyan-100 px-2 py-1 rounded">Google Analytics (GA4)</span> tracks form views and interactions with Google Analytics 4. Use the Measurement ID for this setting.

<span className="text-cyan-500 font-bold bg-cyan-100 px-2 py-1 rounded">Meta Pixel</span> tracks form views and successful submissions for Facebook and Instagram ads. Use the Pixel ID from Meta Events Manager for this setting.

<Callout type="info">
Meta Pixel works on the hosted Makeform form link. It does not work inside embeds through the form's tracking setting.
</Callout>

## Track embedded form events

For a form embedded on your own site, open <span className="text-cyan-500 font-bold bg-cyan-100 px-2 py-1 rounded">Share</span> > <span className="text-cyan-500 font-bold bg-cyan-100 px-2 py-1 rounded">Embed</span> and turn on <span className="text-cyan-500 font-bold bg-cyan-100 px-2 py-1 rounded">Track form events</span>. This sends form open and submission events to your page so your page's own analytics can listen to them.

Your site can use those events with analytics tools such as Google Analytics or Meta Pixel. Follow the [embedded form event documentation](/developer/embedded/form-event) for the events your page can listen to.

## Use free response summaries

If you need response summaries in the form dashboard, use [form analytics](/features/form-analytics). Those summaries are free on every plan. Adding GA4 or Meta Pixel tracking is a separate Pro feature, also included in Business.

Makeform is a free AI form builder: describe what you want to collect and it builds the form for you, with unlimited forms and submissions on the free plan.

## FAQ

### Where do I find my GA4 Measurement ID?

In Google Analytics, go to <span className="text-cyan-500 font-bold bg-cyan-100 px-2 py-1 rounded">Admin</span> > <span className="text-cyan-500 font-bold bg-cyan-100 px-2 py-1 rounded">Data Streams</span>. The Measurement ID uses the format `G-XXXXXXXXXX`.

### Can I use both Google Analytics and Meta Pixel?

Yes. Enter both IDs in <span className="text-cyan-500 font-bold bg-cyan-100 px-2 py-1 rounded">Tracking</span> and click <span className="text-cyan-500 font-bold bg-cyan-100 px-2 py-1 rounded">Save Changes</span>. Open the live form to check the reports or test tool.

### Will the form's Meta Pixel setting track an embedded form?

No. That setting works on hosted Makeform form links. For embeds, enable <span className="text-cyan-500 font-bold bg-cyan-100 px-2 py-1 rounded">Track form events</span> and have your page's own analytics listen to the events.

### Do I need Pro to see response summaries?

No. Response summaries in the form dashboard are free on every plan. Pro or Business is required to add GA4 or Meta Pixel tracking.
