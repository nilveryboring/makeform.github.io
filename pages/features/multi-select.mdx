# Multi Select

Multi select lets respondents choose several options from a dropdown and remove selections individually. You can add it in the form builder for free on every plan.

Makeform is a free AI form builder: describe what you want to collect and it builds the form for you, with unlimited forms and submissions on the free plan.

---

- **Select Multiple Values** – Users can choose more than one option.
- **Removable Selections** – Each selected item can be removed individually.
- **Real-Time Selection Updates** – Selected values appear dynamically in the input field.
- **Search & Filtering** – Users can quickly find options from a long list.
