# Date picker

Date Picker lets respondents select dates from a calendar with customizable date formats and constraints. It is free on every plan and available in the form builder.

Makeform is a free AI form builder: describe what you want to collect and it builds the form for you, with unlimited forms and submissions on the free plan.
