# How to invite team members to collaborate on forms in Makeform

Invite people to share access to your forms from <span className="text-cyan-500 font-bold bg-cyan-100 px-2 py-1 rounded">Members</span> in the dashboard sidebar. Team collaboration requires the team owner to have Pro or Business; invited members can use free Makeform accounts.

**Is it free?** No. The team owner needs Pro or Business, with no seat limit or per-seat price.

## Steps

1. Click <span className="text-cyan-500 font-bold bg-cyan-100 px-2 py-1 rounded">Members</span> in the dashboard sidebar.
2. Click <span className="text-cyan-500 font-bold bg-cyan-100 px-2 py-1 rounded">Invite Member</span>. For your first invitation, click <span className="text-cyan-500 font-bold bg-cyan-100 px-2 py-1 rounded">Invite Your First Team Member</span>; this creates a team workspace and shares your existing forms with the team.
3. In <span className="text-cyan-500 font-bold bg-cyan-100 px-2 py-1 rounded">Invite Team Member</span>, enter the person's <span className="text-cyan-500 font-bold bg-cyan-100 px-2 py-1 rounded">Email Address</span> and choose a <span className="text-cyan-500 font-bold bg-cyan-100 px-2 py-1 rounded">Role</span>. Pro supports <span className="text-cyan-500 font-bold bg-cyan-100 px-2 py-1 rounded">Editor</span>; Business also supports <span className="text-cyan-500 font-bold bg-cyan-100 px-2 py-1 rounded">Admin</span> and <span className="text-cyan-500 font-bold bg-cyan-100 px-2 py-1 rounded">Viewer</span>.
4. Click <span className="text-cyan-500 font-bold bg-cyan-100 px-2 py-1 rounded">Send Invitation</span>.
5. Have the person accept the invitation. They need a Makeform account, but a free account is enough.

## Choose a role

An <span className="text-cyan-500 font-bold bg-cyan-100 px-2 py-1 rounded">Editor</span> can edit forms but cannot manage the team. An <span className="text-cyan-500 font-bold bg-cyan-100 px-2 py-1 rounded">Admin</span> can manage team members and settings. A <span className="text-cyan-500 font-bold bg-cyan-100 px-2 py-1 rounded">Viewer</span> can view forms and submissions.

Admin and Viewer roles require Business. The plan that determines your team's access is the team owner's plan, so an invited member does not need their own paid subscription to join.

<Callout type="info">
Two people cannot edit the same form at the same moment in real time. Team members share access and take turns editing.
</Callout>

## Manage your team

The team area has <span className="text-cyan-500 font-bold bg-cyan-100 px-2 py-1 rounded">Members</span>, <span className="text-cyan-500 font-bold bg-cyan-100 px-2 py-1 rounded">Invites</span>, <span className="text-cyan-500 font-bold bg-cyan-100 px-2 py-1 rounded">Workspaces</span>, and <span className="text-cyan-500 font-bold bg-cyan-100 px-2 py-1 rounded">Settings</span> tabs. To change a member's role or remove them, open <span className="text-cyan-500 font-bold bg-cyan-100 px-2 py-1 rounded">Manage Member</span> and choose <span className="text-cyan-500 font-bold bg-cyan-100 px-2 py-1 rounded">Make Admin</span>, <span className="text-cyan-500 font-bold bg-cyan-100 px-2 py-1 rounded">Make Editor</span>, <span className="text-cyan-500 font-bold bg-cyan-100 px-2 py-1 rounded">Make Viewer</span>, or <span className="text-cyan-500 font-bold bg-cyan-100 px-2 py-1 rounded">Remove from team</span>. The Admin and Viewer choices require Business. Pending invitations can be sent again with <span className="text-cyan-500 font-bold bg-cyan-100 px-2 py-1 rounded">Resend</span>.

Custom workspaces inside a team are available on Pro. Business adds <span className="text-cyan-500 font-bold bg-cyan-100 px-2 py-1 rounded">Add managed account</span> for username/password accounts created by the team, editable <span className="text-cyan-500 font-bold bg-cyan-100 px-2 py-1 rounded">Roles & Permissions</span>, and visibility control to hide or mask sensitive fields from some members.

Makeform is a free AI form builder: describe what you want to collect and it builds the form for you, with unlimited forms and submissions on the free plan.

## FAQ

### Does everyone on my team need Pro?

No. The team owner's plan determines access to collaboration features. Invited people need a Makeform account, and a free account is fine.

### Do I pay for each team member?

No. There is no per-seat price and no seat limit. The plan price covers the team.

### Can I give someone view-only access?

Yes, with the <span className="text-cyan-500 font-bold bg-cyan-100 px-2 py-1 rounded">Viewer</span> role on Business. Viewers can view forms and submissions; Pro supports Editor invitations only.

### Can two people edit a form at the same time?

No. Collaboration shares access to forms, but it does not support simultaneous real-time editing of the same form. Take turns making changes.
