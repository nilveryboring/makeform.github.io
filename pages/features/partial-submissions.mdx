# How to save partial submissions in Makeform

Partial submissions collect answers from people who fill in part of your form but do not click the submit button. Turn the feature on in <span className="text-cyan-500 font-bold bg-cyan-100 px-2 py-1 rounded">Settings</span> > <span className="text-cyan-500 font-bold bg-cyan-100 px-2 py-1 rounded">General</span> on Pro or Business.

**Is it free?** No. Partial submissions require Pro or Business.

## Steps

1. Open your form and click the <span className="text-cyan-500 font-bold bg-cyan-100 px-2 py-1 rounded">Settings</span> icon in the top bar.
2. In <span className="text-cyan-500 font-bold bg-cyan-100 px-2 py-1 rounded">General</span>, turn on <span className="text-cyan-500 font-bold bg-cyan-100 px-2 py-1 rounded">Partial submissions</span>.
3. Click <span className="text-cyan-500 font-bold bg-cyan-100 px-2 py-1 rounded">Save Changes</span>.
4. View partial responses with your submissions.

Use this feature when you want to collect answers from people who started filling in the form but did not finish submitting it. Partial responses appear with your submissions after you enable the setting.

## Limits on partial responses

You cannot export partial submissions through integrations or enable email notifications for them. Keep these limits in mind if your workflow relies on sending responses to another service or notifying someone by email.

Partial responses also do not count toward <span className="text-cyan-500 font-bold bg-cyan-100 px-2 py-1 rounded">Limit responses per option</span>. An unfinished response does not use a place under an option's response limit. See [option response limits](/guides/limit-responses-per-option) for how those limits count responses.

## Partial submissions or save answers for later?

<span className="text-cyan-500 font-bold bg-cyan-100 px-2 py-1 rounded">Save answers for later</span> is a separate, free feature in <span className="text-cyan-500 font-bold bg-cyan-100 px-2 py-1 rounded">Settings</span> > <span className="text-cyan-500 font-bold bg-cyan-100 px-2 py-1 rounded">Behavior</span>. It lets respondents continue later in the same browser. Nothing is sent to you until they submit.

<span className="text-cyan-500 font-bold bg-cyan-100 px-2 py-1 rounded">Partial submissions</span> collects answers from people who did not click the submit button and requires Pro or Business. Choose the setting based on whether you want to collect unfinished answers or let respondents resume in their browser.

## FAQ

### Where do partial responses appear?

Partial responses appear with your submissions. Enable <span className="text-cyan-500 font-bold bg-cyan-100 px-2 py-1 rounded">Partial submissions</span> under <span className="text-cyan-500 font-bold bg-cyan-100 px-2 py-1 rounded">General</span> and click <span className="text-cyan-500 font-bold bg-cyan-100 px-2 py-1 rounded">Save Changes</span> to set up collection.

### Can I send partial responses to an integration?

No. Partial submissions cannot be exported through integrations. This limit applies to the unfinished responses collected by the feature.

### Can I get email notifications for partial submissions?

No. You cannot enable email notifications for partial submissions. Collecting unfinished answers does not make them available for those notifications.

### Do partial submissions count toward option limits?

No. Partial responses do not count toward <span className="text-cyan-500 font-bold bg-cyan-100 px-2 py-1 rounded">Limit responses per option</span>. Those limits count only published responses, including test submissions, and exclude deleted responses.

### Is saving answers for later free?

Yes. <span className="text-cyan-500 font-bold bg-cyan-100 px-2 py-1 rounded">Save answers for later</span> is free and lets respondents continue in the same browser. It sends nothing to you until they submit, while collecting partial submissions requires Pro or Business.
