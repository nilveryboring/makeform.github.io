# Multiple Choice

Multiple choice lets respondents select one option from a list, with an optional “Other” choice for their own answer. You can add it in the form builder for free on every plan.

Makeform is a free AI form builder: describe what you want to collect and it builds the form for you, with unlimited forms and submissions on the free plan.

---

- **Single Selection** – Users can choose only one option at a time.
- **Customizable Options** – Define the list of choices freely.
- **"Other" Option** – Enable an additional "Other" option for user-defined input.
- **Clear Visual Feedback** – The selected option is highlighted dynamically.
- **Perfect for Surveys & Forms** – Ideal for collecting structured user input.
