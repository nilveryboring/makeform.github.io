# Remove Makeform branding

You can remove Makeform branding from forms and notification emails on Pro or Business. Open your form’s <span className="text-cyan-500 font-bold bg-cyan-100 px-2 py-1 rounded">Settings</span> tab and turn off the options you don’t want in the <span className="text-cyan-500 font-bold bg-cyan-100 px-2 py-1 rounded">Branding</span> section.

Makeform is a free AI form builder: describe what you want to collect and it builds the form for you, with unlimited forms and submissions on the free plan.

## How to remove Makeform branding

1. Open your form and go to <span className="text-cyan-500 font-bold bg-cyan-100 px-2 py-1 rounded">Settings</span>.
2. Find the <span className="text-cyan-500 font-bold bg-cyan-100 px-2 py-1 rounded">Branding</span> section.
3. Turn **off** the toggles for the branding you don’t want:
   - <span className="text-cyan-500 font-bold bg-cyan-100 px-2 py-1 rounded">Show loading screen</span> — hides the loading screen.
   - <span className="text-cyan-500 font-bold bg-cyan-100 px-2 py-1 rounded">Show custom message on loading screen</span> — hides custom loading text. Setting custom loading text, up to 100 characters, also requires Pro or Business.
   - <span className="text-cyan-500 font-bold bg-cyan-100 px-2 py-1 rounded">Show 'Powered by' badge</span> — hides the badge on all form views.
   - <span className="text-cyan-500 font-bold bg-cyan-100 px-2 py-1 rounded">Show Makeform link on end page</span> — hides the Makeform link on the end page.
   - <span className="text-cyan-500 font-bold bg-cyan-100 px-2 py-1 rounded">Show Makeform branding in emails</span> — removes the <span className="text-cyan-500 font-bold bg-cyan-100 px-2 py-1 rounded">Powered by Makeform</span> footer from notification emails.
4. Click <span className="text-cyan-500 font-bold bg-cyan-100 px-2 py-1 rounded">Save Changes</span>.

On Free, published forms always show Makeform branding, even if a toggle was changed earlier.

## Embedded forms

The same badge and loading-screen options also appear in <span className="text-cyan-500 font-bold bg-cyan-100 px-2 py-1 rounded">Share</span> > <span className="text-cyan-500 font-bold bg-cyan-100 px-2 py-1 rounded">Embed options</span>. Hiding the badge applies to all form views, including embedded forms.

## Printed forms and downloaded PDFs

In the print preview, open <span className="text-cyan-500 font-bold bg-cyan-100 px-2 py-1 rounded">Customize</span> and select <span className="text-cyan-500 font-bold bg-cyan-100 px-2 py-1 rounded">Remove Makeform branding</span>. This option requires Pro or Business. On Free, printed pages carry a small <span className="text-cyan-500 font-bold bg-cyan-100 px-2 py-1 rounded">Made with Makeform</span> band with a QR code.

## FAQ

### Is removing branding free?

No. Removing Makeform branding requires Pro or Business. Published forms on Free always show branding, even if you changed a toggle earlier.

### Does it remove branding from emails?

Yes. Turn off <span className="text-cyan-500 font-bold bg-cyan-100 px-2 py-1 rounded">Show Makeform branding in emails</span> and click <span className="text-cyan-500 font-bold bg-cyan-100 px-2 py-1 rounded">Save Changes</span> to remove the <span className="text-cyan-500 font-bold bg-cyan-100 px-2 py-1 rounded">Powered by Makeform</span> footer from notification emails.

### Does it apply to embedded forms?

Yes. Hiding the badge applies to all form views, including embeds. You can also find the badge and loading-screen options in <span className="text-cyan-500 font-bold bg-cyan-100 px-2 py-1 rounded">Share</span> > <span className="text-cyan-500 font-bold bg-cyan-100 px-2 py-1 rounded">Embed options</span>.
