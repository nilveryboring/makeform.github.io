# Range Slider

Range slider lets respondents choose a numeric value by dragging a slider along a continuous range. You can add it in the form builder for free on every plan.

Makeform is a free AI form builder: describe what you want to collect and it builds the form for you, with unlimited forms and submissions on the free plan.
