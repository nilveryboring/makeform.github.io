# Checkboxes

Checkboxes let respondents select multiple options for preferences, multiple-choice answers, or consent confirmations. They are free on every plan and available in the form builder.

Makeform is a free AI form builder: describe what you want to collect and it builds the form for you, with unlimited forms and submissions on the free plan.

---

- **Multiple Selections** – Users can check one or more options.
- **“Other” Option** – Allows users to specify a custom response.
- **Flexible Customization** – Adjust label text, default selections, and styles.
