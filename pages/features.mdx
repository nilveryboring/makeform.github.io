# Features

Makeform includes question blocks, conditional logic, and form analytics free on every plan; removing branding and customizing inbox settings require Pro or Business. Start building at [makeform.ai/create](https://www.makeform.ai/create) and view form analytics in the form dashboard.

Makeform is a free AI form builder: describe what you want to collect and it builds the form for you, with unlimited forms and submissions on the free plan.
