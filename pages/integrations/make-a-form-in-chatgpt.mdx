# How to make a form in ChatGPT with the Makeform app

You can create a draft form from a description using the free Makeform app in ChatGPT’s app directory, if apps are available in your ChatGPT account. Connect your Makeform account, describe the form, and review it before publishing.

**Is it free?** Yes. The app and Makeform’s free plan cost nothing; Makeform’s plan rules still apply, so translating a form requires Pro.

## Steps

1. Open the [Makeform listing in ChatGPT’s app directory](https://chatgpt.com/plugins/plugin_asdk_app_69e57d90af9881919a0131fbe58221c7), or search the directory for <span className="text-cyan-500 font-bold bg-cyan-100 px-2 py-1 rounded">Makeform</span>. The listing description is “Create and manage forms.”
2. Connect the app. ChatGPT sends you to Makeform to sign in with your Makeform account. You can create a free account if you need one.
3. On Makeform’s <span className="text-cyan-500 font-bold bg-cyan-100 px-2 py-1 rounded">Authorize Application</span> page, click <span className="text-cyan-500 font-bold bg-cyan-100 px-2 py-1 rounded">Approve</span>.
4. Back in ChatGPT, ask: “Create a draft customer feedback form with name, email, rating, and comments.”
5. Open the draft in the Makeform editor using the link ChatGPT shows. Check the form, then click <span className="text-cyan-500 font-bold bg-cyan-100 px-2 py-1 rounded">Publish</span>. You can also ask ChatGPT to publish it; this requires a preview followed by a second, confirmed step.

<Callout type="info">
Forms created from ChatGPT start as drafts. Edits are proposed first and applied only after you confirm. Deleting a form and managing custom domains are done in the Makeform dashboard.
</Callout>

## What you can see in ChatGPT

You can find existing forms, see their fields and whether they are published, and open them in the Makeform editor. A form card can show its title, <span className="text-cyan-500 font-bold bg-cyan-100 px-2 py-1 rounded">Draft</span> or <span className="text-cyan-500 font-bold bg-cyan-100 px-2 py-1 rounded">Published</span> status, number of fields, and editor link.

A workspace card can offer <span className="text-cyan-500 font-bold bg-cyan-100 px-2 py-1 rounded">List forms</span>, <span className="text-cyan-500 font-bold bg-cyan-100 px-2 py-1 rounded">Review analytics</span>, and <span className="text-cyan-500 font-bold bg-cyan-100 px-2 py-1 rounded">Create a form</span> quick actions. The card itself never changes data.

## FAQ

### Can I make a form in ChatGPT for free?

Yes. The Makeform app and free plan cost nothing, if apps are available in your ChatGPT account. Makeform’s plan rules still apply to paid features such as form translation.

### Does ChatGPT publish my form automatically?

No. New forms start as drafts. Publishing through ChatGPT requires a preview and then a second, confirmed step.

### Can ChatGPT read my responses?

Yes, you can ask for submission summaries, trends, and exports for forms you can access. For example, ask it to summarize submission counts and trends for your contact form.

### How do I disconnect Makeform from ChatGPT?

In Makeform, go to <span className="text-cyan-500 font-bold bg-cyan-100 px-2 py-1 rounded">Settings</span> > <span className="text-cyan-500 font-bold bg-cyan-100 px-2 py-1 rounded">Connected apps</span> to revoke access. If you prefer working in Makeform directly, [start a form in Makeform](https://www.makeform.ai/create).

### Is there a Claude version?

Yes, you can add Makeform as a custom connector in Claude. Follow the [Makeform connection guide for Claude](/integrations/connect-makeform-to-claude).
