# Common tutorials

These use-case tutorials show you how to build quizzes, collect payments and signatures, and create order, booking request, application, RSVP, and registration forms in Makeform. Start by describing your form at [makeform.ai/create](https://www.makeform.ai/create), without an account.

Makeform is a free AI form builder: describe what you want to collect and it builds the form for you, with unlimited forms and submissions on the free plan.
