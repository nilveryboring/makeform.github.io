# How to create a free quiz with Makeform

You can create a quiz for free with Makeform’s standard AI model by describing your questions and answer options in the AI chat. Use the tags below the chatbox to start a prompt, then specify the number and type of questions.

Makeform is a free AI form builder: describe what you want to collect and it builds the form for you, with unlimited forms and submissions on the free plan.
