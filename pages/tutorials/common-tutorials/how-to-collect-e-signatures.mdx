# How to collect a signature in an online form with Makeform

Add a <span className="text-cyan-500 font-bold bg-cyan-100 px-2 py-1 rounded">Signature</span> block from the form editor to collect a signature drawn with a mouse or finger. It is free on every plan, and the signature is saved as an image with the submission.

**Is it free?** Yes. The Signature block and submission PDF export are free on every plan.

## Steps

1. Open your form in the editor, click <span className="text-cyan-500 font-bold bg-cyan-100 px-2 py-1 rounded">+</span>, and choose <span className="text-cyan-500 font-bold bg-cyan-100 px-2 py-1 rounded">Signature</span>.
2. Edit the label, which defaults to <span className="text-cyan-500 font-bold bg-cyan-100 px-2 py-1 rounded">Please sign below</span>.
3. Adjust the optional <span className="text-cyan-500 font-bold bg-cyan-100 px-2 py-1 rounded">Signature Label</span>, <span className="text-cyan-500 font-bold bg-cyan-100 px-2 py-1 rounded">Max Size (KB)</span>, and pen and canvas options. The default maximum size is 500 KB.
4. Put the agreement text above the signature with a <span className="text-cyan-500 font-bold bg-cyan-100 px-2 py-1 rounded">Text</span> block. Add <span className="text-cyan-500 font-bold bg-cyan-100 px-2 py-1 rounded">Short Text Input</span> for a full name and <span className="text-cyan-500 font-bold bg-cyan-100 px-2 py-1 rounded">Date Picker</span> for a date if you need those fields.
5. Publish the form so respondents can draw their signatures.

## What respondents see

Respondents draw on the signature canvas with their mouse or touch screen. They can clear the signature and redraw it before submitting. The Signature block captures a drawn signature, not a typed signature.

Agreement text belongs above the signature field in a Text block. If you also need a full name or date, collect those with the separate fields in the steps above.

## View, export, or print signatures

The signature image appears with the submission and in its PDF. Open <span className="text-cyan-500 font-bold bg-cyan-100 px-2 py-1 rounded">Submissions</span> and choose <span className="text-cyan-500 font-bold bg-cyan-100 px-2 py-1 rounded">Export submission PDF</span> to export the answers and signature image.

You can also attach a PDF to emails. For the owner email, <span className="text-cyan-500 font-bold bg-cyan-100 px-2 py-1 rounded">Attach PDF with answers</span> requires Pro or Business. Respondent email notifications also require Pro or Business; owner email notifications themselves are free.

For a printed form, the printable version includes a signature line where someone can sign with a pen. See [printing a form](/guides/print-a-form) for that workflow.

## FAQ

### Can someone sign on a phone?

Yes. Respondents can draw their signature with a finger on a touch screen. They can also clear it and redraw it.

### Can respondents type their signature?

No. The Signature block captures a signature drawn with a mouse or finger. It saves that drawing as an image with the submission.

### Will the signature appear in the submission PDF?

Yes. The signature image appears in the submission and its PDF. Export it through <span className="text-cyan-500 font-bold bg-cyan-100 px-2 py-1 rounded">Submissions</span> > <span className="text-cyan-500 font-bold bg-cyan-100 px-2 py-1 rounded">Export submission PDF</span>, which is free on every plan.

### Can I receive the answers and signature as an email PDF?

Yes. Enable <span className="text-cyan-500 font-bold bg-cyan-100 px-2 py-1 rounded">Attach PDF with answers</span> for the owner email on Pro or Business. Owner email notifications are free, but attaching the PDF requires a paid plan.
