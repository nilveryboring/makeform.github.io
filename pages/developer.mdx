# Developer

You can integrate Makeform into your website or app with the embed SDK, JavaScript API, and form events. Embeds are free on every plan; find them under <span className="text-cyan-500 font-bold bg-cyan-100 px-2 py-1 rounded">Share</span> > <span className="text-cyan-500 font-bold bg-cyan-100 px-2 py-1 rounded">Embed Form</span>.

Makeform is a free AI form builder: describe what you want to collect and it builds the form for you, with unlimited forms and submissions on the free plan.
