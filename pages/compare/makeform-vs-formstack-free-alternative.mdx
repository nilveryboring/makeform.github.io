# Makeform vs Formstack: a free Formstack alternative (2026)

*By the Makeform editorial team · Last updated November 7, 2026 · Pricing verified November 2026*

**Verdict: Pick Makeform as a free alternative; evaluate Formstack if your requirements extend beyond these form basics.**

Makeform is a free AI form builder: describe what you want to collect and it builds the form for you, with unlimited forms and submissions on the free plan. As of November 2026, Formstack has no free plan and offers a 14-day Forms trial with no credit card required. This comparison covers form limits and pricing; it does not cover enterprise requirements.

## Where Makeform is the better fit

- You need an ongoing free plan for forms and submissions. Makeform Free has unlimited forms and submissions within fair use; as of November 2026, Formstack's free access is a 14-day trial.
- Your needs are covered by AI form drafting, [conditional logic](/features/conditional-logic), [payments](/features/payment), and [file uploads](/features/file-upload). Makeform includes those on Free, subject to its upload limit and standard Stripe processing fees.
- You want to create or edit forms through ChatGPT, Claude, or another MCP client. Makeform provides a hosted MCP server, including publishing with explicit confirmation.
- You need team collaboration at a published lower price. Makeform Pro includes unlimited invited team members for $29/month monthly or $19/month billed yearly; as of November 2026, Formstack Forms is $83/month paid annually with one builder user. Makeform does not offer real-time simultaneous co-editing.

## Where Formstack is the better fit

- Your decision depends on enterprise requirements beyond the form basics documented here. Evaluate Formstack against those requirements directly; as of November 2026, its 14-day Forms trial requires no credit card and offers a way to assess fit before paying.
- You specifically want to evaluate Formstack Documents for AI document generation. As of November 2026, that capability belongs to Documents, not the Forms plan; Makeform's printable forms are not a document-generation product.

## Pricing compared

Makeform offers Free, Pro at $29/month billed monthly or $228/year ($19/month), and Business at $89/month billed monthly or $708/year ($59/month). According to Formstack's pricing page, as of November 2026 there is no free plan; Forms costs $83/month paid annually and includes one builder user, 25 forms, 1,000 submissions per form, and 2 GB storage. Other Formstack plans and Documents pricing are not covered here. Makeform Free retains branding, while Pro adds branding removal, custom domains, and team collaboration; see [plans and pricing](/plans-and-pricing).

## How to switch from Formstack to Makeform

1. Open [Create a form with AI](https://www.makeform.ai/create). Describe the form you want to recreate or paste its questions, including the rules and outcomes it needs.
2. Review and edit by chatting with the AI builder or using the visual editor. Check the form against your actual requirements, including uploads, logic, payments, and any workflow that extends beyond collecting responses.
3. Create a free account to save and publish, then share a hosted link or embed the form. Connect supported integrations such as [Google Sheets](/integrations/google-sheets-integration) or [webhooks](/integrations/webhooks) and verify the resulting workflow before relying on it.

## FAQ

### Is Makeform really free?

Yes, Makeform Free includes unlimited forms, submissions, and questions within fair-use guidelines. It also includes AI form building, conditional logic, and Stripe payments, with standard Stripe processing fees. Free forms display Makeform branding.

### Does Formstack have a free plan?

No: as of November 2026, Formstack offers a 14-day free trial of Forms with no credit card required, rather than an ongoing free plan. Its Forms plan is $83/month paid annually. The trial should not be confused with Makeform's ongoing Free tier.

### Does Formstack Forms include AI document generation?

As of November 2026, AI document generation belongs to Formstack Documents, not the Forms plan. Makeform can lay a form out as a printable document for printing or PDF download, but it is not a document-generation product like Formstack Documents.

### Can I remove branding or invite teammates on Makeform Free?

Both [branding removal](/features/remove-makeform-branding) and team collaboration require Makeform Pro. Pro includes unlimited invited team members and custom domains. Makeform does not support real-time simultaneous co-editing.

### Can I use Makeform from ChatGPT or Claude?

Yes. Makeform is in [ChatGPT's app directory](https://chatgpt.com/plugins/plugin_asdk_app_69e57d90af9881919a0131fbe58221c7); in Claude you add it as a custom connector (mcp.makeform.ai). Both use OAuth account linking and Makeform's hosted MCP server. It supports form creation, editing, and submission work, with explicit confirmation required for publishing. You can also start directly at [Create a form with AI](https://www.makeform.ai/create) without an account, then create a free account to save and publish.
