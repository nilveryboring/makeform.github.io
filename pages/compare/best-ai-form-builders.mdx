### 1. Makeform: best for AI-first building

Describe what you need in one sentence and Makeform builds the whole form, then lets you revise it by chatting or editing visually. You can start a draft without an account and use the same builder for scored quizzes, personality quizzes, placement tests, and printable documents.

**Free plan:** Unlimited forms, submissions and questions within fair-use guidelines; AI generates up to 50 questions in one go, with more questions addable manually.

**Paid from:** Pro is $29/month monthly or $19/month billed yearly ($228/year).

**Good:**

- Free logic, calculations, Stripe payments and uploads up to 20 MB per file.
- Printable forms and quizzes with answer keys, available to print or download as PDFs.
- No account needed to start an AI draft at [Create a form with AI](https://www.makeform.ai/create).

**Watch out:**

- Saving, publishing and managing forms require a free account.
- Branding removal, custom domains, collaboration and premium AI models require Pro.
- There is no native mobile app or real-time simultaneous co-editing.

See [Makeform plans and pricing](/plans-and-pricing).

### 2. Tally: best for free AI imports

As of November 2026, Tally creates forms from prompts, screenshots or PDFs and offers Ask AI edits on every plan. It is a close second because its free plan has similar form and submission allowances to Makeform.

**Free plan:** Unlimited forms and submissions within fair-use guidelines; AI included; uploads up to 10 MB per file.

**Paid from:** Pro is $29/month monthly or $24/month billed yearly.

**Good:**

- Screenshot and PDF input are included in free AI creation.
- Free calculations, conditional logic, payments and signatures.

**Watch out:**

- Removing Tally branding requires Pro.
- Free uploads have a smaller per-file allowance than Makeform's 20 MB.

Read [Makeform vs Tally](/compare/makeform-vs-tally-free-alternative).

### 3. Jotform: best for native integration choice

As of November 2026, Jotform's free AI Form Generator lets you refine a form through chat, with usage governed by your form plan. Its advertised 150+ integrations make it a stronger fit than Makeform when native integration breadth matters most.

**Free plan:** Starter includes 5 active forms, 100 monthly submissions, 10,000 monthly form views, 100 fields per form and 100 MB upload space.

**Paid from:** Bronze is $39/month monthly or $34/month billed annually.

**Good:**

- AI generation and conversational refinement.
- More native integrations than Makeform.

**Watch out:**

- The free submission and active-form limits can constrain ongoing collection.
- Free forms carry Jotform branding; Bronze allows its removal.

Read [Makeform vs Jotform](/compare/makeform-vs-jotform-free-alternative).

### 4. Fillout: best for AI forms with scheduling

As of November 2026, Fillout's Form Agent turns a description or pasted questions into fields, pages and logic, then accepts follow-up changes. Its free plan pairs form collection with scheduling, payments, PDF generation and scoring.

**Free plan:** Unlimited forms and seats, 1,000 responses/month, uploads up to 20 MB per file and the Fillout badge.

**Paid from:** Starter is $15/month billed annually ($180/year).

**Good:**

- The agent builds form structure and logic together.
- Free scheduling, calculations and resume-in-progress forms.

**Watch out:**

- Free responses stop at a monthly allowance of 1,000.
- Removing all branding requires Pro at $40/month billed annually; custom domains require Business.

Read [Makeform vs Fillout](/compare/makeform-vs-fillout-free-alternative).

### 5. Typeform: best for small-volume AI forms

As of November 2026, Typeform's AI chatbot builds forms on Free, which also includes logic, scores and variables. It is an option for trying AI creation with a small audience, but its 10-response monthly allowance keeps it below the more generous free plans here.

**Free plan:** Unlimited typeforms, with 10 responses/month shared across all forms.

**Paid from:** Basic is $28/month billed yearly or $39/month monthly.

**Good:**

- AI form building is available on Free.
- Free logic includes scores and variables.

**Watch out:**

- File uploads, payments and knowledge quiz mode are paid-only.
- Branding removal requires Plus at $56/month billed yearly.

Read [Makeform vs Typeform](/compare/makeform-vs-typeform-free-alternative).

### 6. Paperform: best for trying AI workflows on Free

As of November 2026, Paperform includes both AI form creation and an AI workflow builder on its free forever plan. That combination is worth considering for a small collection workflow, although the free submission allowance is modest.

**Free plan:** 30 submissions/month, 100 MB file upload storage and Paperform branding.

**Paid from:** Pro is $59/month monthly or $49/month billed annually ($588/year).

**Good:**

- AI creation and workflow building are included on Free.
- Pro provides unlimited submissions within fair-use guidelines.

**Watch out:**

- Free submissions are limited to 30 per month.
- Removing branding requires Pro.

Read [Makeform vs Paperform](/compare/makeform-vs-paperform-free-alternative).

### 7. Google Forms: best for classroom quizzes

As of November 2026, Google Forms offers answer keys, automatic grading and a choice of immediate or delayed grade release. Those classroom controls are stronger than Makeform's, but Gemini's Help me create a form requires an eligible paid Google Workspace or Google AI plan.

**Free plan:** Available with a Google account; the cited pages state no response cap.

**Paid from:** Google Workspace Business Starter is $7/user/month billed monthly; check eligibility separately for Gemini form creation.

**Good:**

- Answer keys, points and feedback for quizzes.
- Grades can be released after manual review.

**Watch out:**

- Gemini form creation is not part of the ordinary free-account offering.
- File upload questions require respondents to sign in to Google.
- Design and branding options are limited compared with dedicated form builders.

Read [Makeform vs Google Forms](/compare/makeform-vs-google-forms-free-alternative).

## Which one should you pick?

- If you want one-sentence creation, chat edits and printable outputs, pick Makeform.
- If you want free AI creation from a PDF of an existing form, pick Tally.
- If you need a broader native integration selection, pick Jotform.
- If you need scheduling alongside form logic and scoring, pick Fillout.
- If you need classroom grading and delayed grade release, pick Google Forms; treat Gemini eligibility as a separate decision.

## FAQ

### Which AI form builder is best for free use?

Makeform is our pick for building whole forms, quizzes and printable documents from a description. Tally is a close alternative and also lets you start from a screenshot or a PDF on its free plan as of November 2026.

### Can I start without creating an account?

You can start a Makeform AI draft without an account. Saving, publishing and managing the form require a free account.

### Does Google Forms include free Gemini form creation?

As of November 2026, Help me create a form requires an eligible Google Workspace or Google AI plan. Free Google Forms access alone does not include that feature.

### Can I edit an AI-generated form afterward?

Makeform supports chat edits and visual editing. As of November 2026, Tally offers Ask AI, Jotform supports chat refinement, and Fillout's Form Agent accepts follow-up changes.

### Can AI build a quiz with an answer key?

Makeform can build a scored knowledge quiz and lay it out as a printable quiz with an answer key. It does not offer a timer, certificates or a teacher-controlled release-grades-later flow.
