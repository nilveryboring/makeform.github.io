# Compare Makeform with other form builders

*By the Makeform editorial team · Last updated November 7, 2026 · Pricing verified November 2026*

Makeform is a free AI form builder: describe what you want to collect and it builds the form for you, with unlimited forms and submissions on the free plan. Use these comparisons to weigh free-plan limits and features as of November 2026, including where another tool is the better fit.

Makeform's free AI builder generates up to 50 questions in one go; you can add more questions manually. Branding removal, custom domains and collaboration need Pro, and Makeform has fewer native integrations than Jotform, no native mobile app and no real-time simultaneous co-editing.

Makeform is in [ChatGPT's app directory](https://chatgpt.com/plugins/plugin_asdk_app_69e57d90af9881919a0131fbe58221c7); in Claude you add it as a custom connector (mcp.makeform.ai). Tally is a close free-plan competitor, and its free AI can also start from a PDF of an existing form. Google Forms is a better fit for classroom quizzes that need grade release after manual review, while Fillout includes unlimited seats on Free. These competitor features are as of November 2026.

## FAQ

### Is Makeform really free?

Yes. The $0 plan includes unlimited forms, submissions and questions within fair-use guidelines, plus AI form building, conditional logic, calculations, file uploads up to 20 MB per file and Stripe payments with no added Makeform fee; standard Stripe processing fees apply. Free forms display Makeform branding.

### Which competitor is closest to Makeform's free plan?

Tally is a close alternative as of November 2026, with unlimited forms and submissions within fair-use guidelines, free AI, calculations, logic and payments. Tally's AI also accepts a PDF of an existing form; Makeform offers a larger free per-file upload allowance, quizzes built from a sentence and printable documents.

### How should I choose between the free plans?

Match the response allowance, upload requirements and quiz or workflow features to the work you need to do. As of November 2026, Google Forms stands out for classroom grade release, Fillout for free seats and scheduling, and Jotform for broader native integration choice; Makeform and Tally offer unlimited free form submissions within fair use.
