# Makeform vs Google Forms: a free Google Forms alternative (2026)

*By the Makeform editorial team · Last updated November 7, 2026 · Pricing verified November 2026*

**Verdict: Choose Makeform for free AI form creation and more design options; choose Google Forms for quizzes that need manual review before grades are released.**

Makeform is a free AI form builder: describe what you want to collect and it builds the form for you, with unlimited forms and submissions on the free plan. As of November 2026, Google Forms is free with a Google account, while Gemini's Help me create a form feature requires an eligible Google Workspace or Google AI plan. Google's cited pages do not state a response cap, so response volume alone does not establish an advantage here.

## Where Makeform is the better fit

- You want to describe a form and keep editing it with AI on a free plan. As of November 2026, Google requires an eligible Workspace or Google AI plan for its Gemini form-creation feature.
- You need free [payments](/features/payment), signatures, or [conditional logic](/features/conditional-logic) as part of your form workflow. Makeform documents these on Free, alongside integrations such as [Google Sheets](/integrations/google-sheets-integration) and webhooks.
- You want a personality quiz with a separate result ending for each outcome, or a placement quiz with one ending per level. Makeform's AI builder supports both.
- You want more control over presentation and are willing to pay for it. Makeform Pro includes custom CSS, your own logo, branding removal, and a [custom domain](/features/custom-domain); as of November 2026, Google Forms has comparatively limited design and branding options.

## Where Google Forms is the better fit

- You need to review quiz answers before releasing grades. As of November 2026, Google Forms supports immediate grade release or release after manual review; Makeform does not offer the latter workflow.
- Your quiz needs built-in automatic grading for choice, short-answer, and grid questions. As of November 2026, Google Forms documents all three, plus points and feedback. Makeform marks typed answers by exact spelling while ignoring capitals, and collects long open answers unscored for you to mark.

## Pricing compared

Makeform offers Free, Pro at $29/month or $228/year ($19/month), and Business at $89/month or $708/year ($59/month). Pro includes branding removal, custom domains, and team collaboration; Free retains Makeform branding. As of November 2026, Google Forms is free with a Google account, and Google's Workspace pricing lists Business Starter at $7/user/month billed monthly. Gemini form creation requires an eligible Workspace or Google AI plan; check Google's plan pages for which plans qualify. See Makeform's [plans and pricing](/plans-and-pricing) for its plan boundaries.

## How to switch from Google Forms to Makeform

1. Open [Create a form with AI](https://www.makeform.ai/create) and describe your existing form or paste its questions. Start the draft without an account.
2. Review and edit by chatting with the AI or using the visual editor. For quizzes, check correct answers, score endings, and answer review; account for Makeform's lack of a release-grades-later workflow.
3. Create a free account to save and publish, then share the hosted link or embed the form. Connect [Google Sheets](/integrations/google-sheets-integration) or another supported integration for your new responses.

## FAQ

### Is Makeform really free?

Yes: Free includes unlimited forms, submissions, and questions within fair-use guidelines. AI building, payments, file uploads up to 20 MB per file, and conditional logic are included. Branding removal, custom domains, and collaboration require Pro.

### Is Google Forms free, and does it have a response cap?

As of November 2026, Google Forms is free to use with a Google account. The cited Google pages do not state a response cap, so this comparison does not assign one.

### Does Google Forms include free AI form creation?

As of November 2026, Help me create a form with Gemini requires an eligible Google Workspace or Google AI plan. Makeform includes AI creation and chat-based editing on Free, with up to 50 questions created in one draft; you can add more questions yourself.

### Which builder is better for classroom quizzes?

As of November 2026, Google Forms is the better fit when you need to release grades after manual review or automatically grade grid questions. Makeform supports AI-built knowledge quizzes, score endings, and printable quizzes with answer keys. It has no timer, certificates, or teacher flow for releasing grades later.

### Do Google Forms uploads require respondents to sign in?

As of November 2026, Google's help documentation says file upload questions require respondents to sign in to a Google account. Makeform's documented Free upload allowance is 20 MB per file, rising to 1 GB per file on Pro.

### Can I remove Makeform branding or connect it to an AI assistant?

Branding removal requires Makeform Pro. Makeform is in ChatGPT's app directory; in Claude you add it as a custom connector (mcp.makeform.ai). Its MCP server supports creating and editing forms, working with submissions, and publishing with explicit confirmation.
