# Makeform vs Typeform: a free Typeform alternative (2026)

*By the Makeform editorial team · Last updated November 7, 2026 · Pricing verified November 2026*

**Verdict: Choose Makeform for ongoing free response collection; choose Typeform if you want one-click setup from Claude's connector directory and only need a few responses a month.**

Makeform is a free AI form builder: describe what you want to collect and it builds the form for you, with unlimited forms and submissions on the free plan. As of November 2026, Typeform Free includes unlimited forms but only 10 responses per month across them. Both offer AI form building, so the response allowance and access to particular question types matter more than the presence of AI alone.

## Where Makeform is the better fit

- You expect more than 10 submissions a month and want to stay on a free plan. As of November 2026, Typeform's free allowance is shared across all your forms; Makeform has unlimited submissions within fair-use guidelines.
- Your form needs [file uploads](/features/file-upload), [payments](/features/payment), or signatures on Free. As of November 2026, Typeform places those question types on paid plans.
- You want free [conditional logic](/features/conditional-logic) alongside redirects, reCAPTCHA, and AI-built knowledge quizzes. As of November 2026, Typeform includes free logic, but its redirects, reCAPTCHA, and knowledge quiz mode require paid plans.
- You want to remove branding at a lower listed price. Makeform Pro starts at $19/month billed yearly; as of November 2026, Typeform Plus includes branding removal at $56/month billed yearly.

## Where Typeform is the better fit

- Your workflow starts in Claude's connector directory. As of November 2026, Typeform is listed there and in ChatGPT's directory, with its MCP server available on every plan, including Free. Makeform is in [ChatGPT's app directory](https://chatgpt.com/plugins/plugin_asdk_app_69e57d90af9881919a0131fbe58221c7); in Claude you add it as a custom connector (mcp.makeform.ai).

## Pricing compared

Makeform has Free, Pro at $29/month or $228/year ($19/month), and Business at $89/month or $708/year ($59/month). As of November 2026, Typeform's pricing page lists yearly-billed Basic at $28/month with 100 responses/month and one user, Plus at $56/month with 1,000 responses/month and three users, and Business at $91/month with 10,000 responses/month and five users. Typeform Basic is $39/month on monthly billing; Plus includes branding removal. Makeform Pro includes branding removal, custom domains, and invitations for unlimited team members, but Makeform has no real-time simultaneous co-editing. See [plans and pricing](/plans-and-pricing).

## How to switch from Typeform to Makeform

1. Start at [Create a form with AI](https://www.makeform.ai/create) and describe your Typeform or paste its questions. You can begin the AI draft without an account.
2. Review the questions and ask the AI to adjust the wording, logic, or ending, or make changes visually. Check scoring and redirects against the behavior you want.
3. Save and publish with a free account, then share a hosted link or embed the form. Connect your submission destination, such as [Google Sheets](/integrations/google-sheets-integration), and configure [email notifications](/features/email-notifications).

## FAQ

### Is Makeform really free for more than 10 responses?

Yes: Makeform Free includes unlimited submissions within fair-use guidelines. Free forms retain Makeform branding, and features such as custom domains, team collaboration, and partial submissions require Pro.

### Is Typeform free?

As of November 2026, Typeform Free includes unlimited forms and 10 responses per month across all forms. It also includes the AI form-building chatbot and logic with scores and variables.

### Can I take payments or collect uploads for free?

Makeform Free includes Stripe payments and uploads up to 20 MB per file. Makeform adds no payment fee of its own, but standard Stripe processing fees apply. As of November 2026, Typeform's payment and file upload questions require a paid plan.

### Which paid plan removes branding?

Makeform Pro removes branding for $29/month on monthly billing or $19/month billed yearly. As of November 2026, Typeform Plus includes branding removal at $56/month billed yearly; the cheaper Basic plan does not include it.

### Can I build forms from ChatGPT or Claude?

Yes. Makeform is in [ChatGPT's app directory](https://chatgpt.com/plugins/plugin_asdk_app_69e57d90af9881919a0131fbe58221c7); in Claude you add it as a custom connector (mcp.makeform.ai). It signs in with OAuth and shows interactive Makeform cards in ChatGPT. As of November 2026, Typeform's MCP server is available on every plan and Typeform is listed in both assistant directories. Makeform requires an explicit confirmation step to publish through MCP.

### Can Makeform create a graded quiz?

Yes: it can build a knowledge quiz from a description, score correct options automatically, and show a percentage score with optional answer review after submission. It also supports pass marks and grade bands, but has no timer or teacher flow for releasing grades later. As of November 2026, Typeform's knowledge quiz mode is paid, although its Free plan includes scores and variables.
