# 7 best free Google Forms alternatives in 2026

*By the Makeform editorial team · Last updated November 7, 2026 · Pricing verified November 2026*

**Short answer: Makeform is our top free Google Forms alternative for AI building and generous collection limits; Tally is a close second with a very similar free plan.**

This ranking weighs free-plan generosity alongside useful features, with competitor facts stated as of November 2026. Google Forms remains a strong choice for classroom grading, especially when you need to release grades after review. Makeform is a free AI form builder: describe what you want to collect and it builds the form for you, with unlimited forms and submissions on the free plan.

## How we picked

- Response allowances, including whether they apply monthly or per form.
- Free forms, questions, upload limits and seats where stated.
- Useful free features such as AI, logic, calculations and payments.
- Branding restrictions and the cost of removing them.
- Clear reasons to keep using Google Forms or choose another competitor.

Makeform publishes this page, so we list our own limits and link every source.

### 1. Makeform: best for AI building with generous free limits

Makeform combines unlimited free collection with AI that builds a whole form from a description and accepts chat edits. It also produces scored, personality and placement quizzes and printable documents, making it our first choice when you want more than a basic collection form.

**Free plan:** Unlimited forms, submissions and questions within fair-use guidelines; uploads up to 20 MB per file; AI generates up to 50 questions in one go.

**Paid from:** Pro is $29/month monthly or $19/month billed yearly ($228/year).

**Good:**

- Free conditional logic, calculations, answer piping, hidden fields and multi-page forms.
- Stripe payments with no added Makeform fee; standard Stripe processing fees apply.
- Free Google Sheets, Notion, Slack, Airtable, Zapier, Make and webhook integrations.

**Watch out:**

- Free forms display Makeform branding; removing it, using custom domains and collaborating require Pro.
- Respondent email notifications and premium AI models require Pro.
- Google Forms has a teacher-controlled delayed grade release flow that Makeform lacks.

Start a draft without an account at [Create a form with AI](https://www.makeform.ai/create); saving and publishing require a free account. See [Makeform vs Google Forms](/compare/makeform-vs-google-forms-free-alternative).

### 2. Tally: best for free AI imports

As of November 2026, Tally is a close competitor with unlimited forms and submissions within fair-use guidelines. Its free AI accepts a screenshot or PDF as well as a prompt.

**Free plan:** Unlimited forms and submissions within fair use; file uploads up to 10 MB per file; AI creation and Ask AI edits on every plan.

**Paid from:** Pro is $29/month monthly or $24/month billed yearly.

**Good:**

- Free payments, signatures, conditional logic and calculations.
- Screenshot and PDF input for AI form creation.
- Free self email notifications and redirect on completion.

**Watch out:**

- Removing Tally branding requires Pro.
- Free uploads are limited to 10 MB per file, compared with Makeform's 20 MB.

Read [Makeform vs Tally](/compare/makeform-vs-tally-free-alternative).

### 3. Fillout: best for shared form work and scheduling

As of November 2026, Fillout offers unlimited free forms and seats alongside a 1,000-response monthly allowance. Its combination of scheduling, payments, PDF generation and scoring earns it third place even though submissions are capped.

**Free plan:** 1,000 responses/month, unlimited forms and seats, 20 MB maximum file size and the Fillout badge.

**Paid from:** Starter is $15/month billed annually ($180/year), with 2,000 responses/month.

**Good:**

- Free scheduling, payments, conditional logic, calculations and scoring.
- Free PDF generation and resume-in-progress forms.
- Unlimited free seats are an advantage over Makeform's Pro collaboration requirement.

**Watch out:**

- Free collection has a monthly response cap.
- Removing all branding requires Pro at $40/month billed annually; unlimited responses and a custom domain require Business at $75/month billed annually.

Read [Makeform vs Fillout](/compare/makeform-vs-fillout-free-alternative).

### 4. Microsoft Forms: best for quizzes with a personal Microsoft account

As of November 2026, Microsoft Forms supports quizzes with points, correct answers and per-answer messages. Its free personal-account limit applies per form, making it worth considering for separate, modest-size quizzes rather than comparing its allowance directly with a monthly submission pool.

**Free plan:** Personal Microsoft accounts allow up to 200 responses per form, 400 forms and 200 questions per form.

**Paid from:** A paid personal Microsoft account raises the limit to 1,000 responses per form (Microsoft 365 prices are not covered here).

**Good:**

- Built-in quiz points and correct answers.
- A per-form response allowance instead of the monthly pool used by several tools here.

**Watch out:**

- Personal free accounts have a 200-response limit per form.
- Copilot in Forms needs a Microsoft 365 account and a Copilot license.
- Work/school accounts have different limits; their allowance of up to 5,000,000 responses per form does not describe a free personal account.

Read [Makeform vs Microsoft Forms](/compare/makeform-vs-microsoft-forms-free-alternative).

### 5. Jotform: best for native integration choice

As of November 2026, Jotform offers free AI generation with chat refinement and a quiz maker with automatic grading. It advertises 150+ integrations, a broader native selection than Makeform, but its free active-form and submission caps hold it back in a generosity-focused ranking.

**Free plan:** Starter includes 5 active forms, 100 monthly submissions, 10,000 monthly form views, 100 fields per form and 100 MB upload space.

**Paid from:** Bronze is $39/month monthly or $34/month billed annually, with 25 forms and 1,000 submissions/month.

**Good:**

- Free AI Form Generator with conversational refinement.
- A broad selection of native integrations.

**Watch out:**

- Form, field, view, storage and monthly submission limits all matter on Starter.
- Free forms show Jotform branding; Bronze allows its removal.

Read [Makeform vs Jotform](/compare/makeform-vs-jotform-free-alternative).

### 6. Paperform: best for small AI workflows

As of November 2026, Paperform has a free forever plan that includes AI form creation and its AI workflow builder. Those features make it useful to consider for low-volume work, though 30 monthly submissions leave less room than the tools above.

**Free plan:** 30 submissions/month and 100 MB file upload storage, with Paperform branding.

**Paid from:** Pro is $59/month monthly or $49/month billed annually ($588/year).

**Good:**

- AI form creation and workflow building on Free.
- A free forever plan alongside a 7-day trial of paid plans.

**Watch out:**

- Free submissions are limited to 30 per month.
- Branding removal requires Pro, which includes unlimited submissions within fair use and 10 GB storage.

Read [Makeform vs Paperform](/compare/makeform-vs-paperform-free-alternative).

### 7. Typeform: best for very small collection needs

As of November 2026, Typeform includes AI chatbot form building and logic with scores and variables on Free. It ranks last here because only 10 responses per month are available across all forms, and several collection features require payment.

**Free plan:** Unlimited typeforms, sharing 10 responses/month.

**Paid from:** Basic is $28/month billed yearly or $39/month monthly; Basic includes 100 responses/month.

**Good:**

- Free AI form building.
- Free logic, scores and variables.

**Watch out:**

- File upload, payment and signature questions are paid-only.
- Knowledge quiz mode, redirects and reCAPTCHA are paid-only.
- Removing branding requires Plus at $56/month billed yearly.

Read [Makeform vs Typeform](/compare/makeform-vs-typeform-free-alternative).

## Which one should you pick?

- If you want AI creation, printable outputs and unlimited free submissions within fair use, pick Makeform.
- If you want similar free collection allowances and AI that can start from a PDF, pick Tally.
- If you need free seats and scheduling within 1,000 responses/month, pick Fillout.
- If you use a personal Microsoft account for modest-size quizzes, pick Microsoft Forms.
- If native integration breadth matters more than free volume, pick Jotform.
- If classroom answer keys and delayed grade release are the priority, keep Google Forms.

## FAQ

### Which free alternative is closest to Makeform's limits?

As of November 2026, Tally also offers unlimited free forms and submissions within fair-use guidelines. Its free upload limit is 10 MB per file, compared with Makeform's 20 MB.

### Does Google Forms have a response cap?

The cited Google pages do not state a response cap, so we do not assign one. Google Forms is free with a Google account as of November 2026.

### Can I remove branding for free?

Makeform requires Pro for branding removal. As of November 2026, the free plans of Tally, Fillout, Jotform, Paperform and Typeform also retain their branding; the cited sources do not specify a branding-removal tier for Microsoft Forms.

### How do I move my questions into Makeform?

Describe the existing form or paste its questions into the AI builder, then review and edit the result by chatting. After creating a free account, publish or embed it and connect the integrations you need.

### Are free uploads measured the same way across tools?

No. As of November 2026, Makeform and Fillout specify 20 MB per file and Tally specifies 10 MB per file, while Jotform and Paperform list 100 MB of upload storage on Free.
